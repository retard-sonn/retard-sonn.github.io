/scope · CLEARANCE 4

Scope.

SCOPE

Authorized targets only. Written permission, defined boundaries, a report at the end.

OUT OF SCOPE

Everything else. There is no interesting version of this rule that I get to bend.

Penetration testing and bug bounty hunting are the niche I chose. I hunt the slow way: read the program brief, map the surface, stay inside the lines I was given, and write the finding up so somebody else can reproduce it without me in the room. The report is the deliverable — the shell is just the evidence.
